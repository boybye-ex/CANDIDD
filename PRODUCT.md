# candidd

## What it is

**candidd** is a photography portfolio for candid and documentary work. The site exists so visitors experience the photographs first — not a resume, blog, or booking funnel.

## Audience

People deciding whether this photographer’s eye fits a shoot, commission, or collaboration. They arrive for the images; proof is in the frame, not claims.

## Mode

**Experience** — the photographs are the product. Interface chrome stays quiet and secondary. Contact is a quiet close.

## v1 scope

- Single-page Experience: scatter gallery + brand overlay
- Local portfolio assets, dark cinematic world

## v2 scope

- Local portfolio files under `public/portfolio` (swap in your own frames)
- `#contact` inquiry form (name, email, shoot type, date, location, message, reference set/frame)
- Email delivery via Web3Forms (`WEB3FORMS_ACCESS_KEY` in `.env`)
- Clickable scatter frames → lightbox → “Inquire about this look” pre-fills reference

## Out of scope

Blog, CMS, auth, booking calendar, payments, long About page.

## Brand

- Name: **candidd** (lowercase)
- One-line: candid photography
- Success: visitor remembers the photos and the name, and can send a clear inquiry.
